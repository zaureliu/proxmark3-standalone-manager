# Code of conduct

This project discusses radio and access-control hardware. That subject
requires restraint.

## Expected behavior

- Be precise and respectful in issues, reviews, and discussions.
- Experiment only on equipment, tags, and systems you own or are allowed to
  test. Do not ask for or publish data captured from someone else's tag or
  installation.
- Do not post keys, dumps, serial numbers, or other identifiers.
- Credit the Iceman firmware, ProxSpace, Qt, and other third-party work.
  Do not present this application as their product.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, or disclosure of someone's private
  information.
- Requests for help attacking a system, credential, or facility you do not
  own and are not authorized to test.
- Uploading malware, stolen dumps, or firmware redistributed against its
  license.

## Reports

Contact the project owner privately. Use a private report rather than a
public issue when the repository is public. The owner will decide what to
do. No personal contact address is published in this file.

This text is specific to this project. It is not the Contributor Covenant.
